Spleeve Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

Spleeve does not collect personal data, track users, or upload your audio files or artwork to our servers.

Information We Collect

Spleeve does not collect personal information, analytics, diagnostics, advertising identifiers, usage data, or contact information.

Audio Files and Artwork

Audio files, metadata, and artwork you import into Spleeve are processed on your device. Spleeve does not upload your files, metadata, edits, or exports to a Spleeve server.

Exports and Sharing

When you export or share a file, it is handled by iOS and by the destination you choose, such as Files or another app. Those destinations may have their own privacy practices.

Third-Party Services

Spleeve does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Spleeve does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact
